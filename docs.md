The core system [risk vs reward]:
0. Bubbles mechanics
-they r like coins but stay on the ground for only shorter period of time
-gotta jump and grab them

1. Bubbles are unbanked
-Bullet hit the player, bubbles pop out and fly away(20-30%)
-Try to regrab them or leave them
-u will not die if u can 0 bubbles, we have health bar for that

2. Pressure Boxes
-swollen canister type thingy, ready to blow up which explodes after x hits
-after explosion a gas in vicinity that can damage the player, he can choose to go in for the bubbles or not
-if inside blast radius? death : collect bubbles residue from explosion;

3. Bottling Points(checking points)
-bank ur bubbles here and respawn if died
-loose unbanked bubbles
-u can choose to bank or not

4. Basic Junctions
-one path risky other path easier

5. Inflation
-cost increase with each purchase (not decided yet)

6. Foam Overflow
-for vertical climbing types, foam rises from below
-changing paths for bubbles cost time

7. Pressure valve
-increases the abilites of the guards
-player can choose the difficulty. Higher difficulty higher the reward

8. Shortcut Purchase
-Pay N bubbles for shortcut or new area unlocked
-we have time to design fun environments

9. Win condition

 Contained / Leaked / Overflowed
